# App Site Map

## Plan
- **Purpose**: Users plan upcoming activities and events.
- **Features**:
  - Add/edit/delete activities (title, description, importance)
  - Link to Google Calendar events
  - Optionally schedule a photo reminder

## Today
- **Purpose**: Users see and act on today’s tasks and events.
- **Features**:
  - List of today’s planned activities
  - Mark tasks as done or reschedule
  - Upload or view related photos

## Journal
- **Purpose**: Reflect on completed activities and events.
- **Features**:
  - View completed activities in chronological order
  - Browse photos linked to each completed activity
  - Filter by date range or activity tag

## Photos
- **Purpose**: Browse and manage Google Photos.
- **Features**:
  - Grid view by date or album
  - Link/unlink photos to activities/events
  - Quick preview and full-screen view

## Settings
- **Purpose**: Manage account, integrations, and preferences.
- **Features**:
  - Login/Logout
  - Calendar & Tasks integration toggles
  - Theme and notification settings
